Community Action Grants

Community Grants

Funding of up to K10,000 per project for local MSMEs, youth and women's associations, churches, and NGOs delivering community impact across the National Capital District.

Who can apply

  • Local MSMEs
  • Youth & women's associations
  • Churches
  • Registered NGOs

What's funded

  • Community events & programs
  • Small business development
  • Youth & skills initiatives
  • Local infrastructure improvements
