Building Authority

Building Approvals

Submit plans, get inspections, and receive certificates through the NCDC Building Authority — for residential, commercial, and public works across the district.

Who needs approval

  • New construction of any kind
  • Extensions or structural alterations
  • Change of building use
  • Demolition of existing structures

What you'll need

  • Architectural plans (certified)
  • Proof of land title or lease
  • Structural engineer's certification
  • Environmental clearance, where applicable
Process

How an application moves through approval.

01

Submit application

Lodge plans and supporting documents with the Building Authority office or online portal.

02

Technical review

Engineers review structural, safety, and zoning compliance.

03

Site inspection

An inspector visits the site to confirm details match the application.

04

Approval or feedback

You receive approval, or specific feedback to address and resubmit.

05

Certificate issued

Once compliant, a building certificate is issued and construction may proceed.
