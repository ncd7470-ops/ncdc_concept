Trade & Licensing

Business Licensing

Trading licenses, market permits, and renewals for MSMEs and larger businesses operating across the National Capital District.

License categories

  • Retail & wholesale trading
  • Food & beverage outlets
  • Professional services
  • Market & street vendor permits

What you'll need

  • IPA business registration certificate
  • TIN certificate
  • Proof of premises (lease or title)
  • Health clearance, for food businesses
Process

Getting licensed to trade.

01

Register with IPA

Business must first be registered with the Investment Promotion Authority.

02

Lodge license application

Submit business details and premises documentation to NCDC.

03

Inspection, where required

Food and health-related businesses receive a premises inspection.

04

License issued

Once approved, your trading license is issued for the license period.

05

Annual renewal

Licenses are renewed yearly ahead of the expiry date.
