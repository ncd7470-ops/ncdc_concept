Civil & Identity Registry

Civil & Identity Registry

Register births, marriages, and deaths, and get support with identity documentation for National Capital District residents.

Services offered

  • Birth registration & certificates
  • Marriage registration & certificates
  • Death registration & certificates
  • Certified copy requests

What you'll need

  • Valid identification
  • Hospital or witness documentation
  • Completed registry form
  • Registration fee payment
Process

Registering a life event.

01

Collect documentation

Gather hospital records, witness statements, or other required proof.

02

Complete the registry form

Available at the registry office or as a downloadable form.

03

Submit in person

Lodge the form and documents at the Civil & Identity Registry counter.

04

Verification

Registry staff verify the details against submitted documentation.

05

Certificate issued

A certified copy of the registration is issued, typically within 5 working days.
