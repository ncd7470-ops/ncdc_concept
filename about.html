About the Commission

A governing body for the city, established by the NCDC Act.

The National Capital District Commission is the administrative authority responsible for municipal services across Port Moresby.

Mandate

Legislating, managing, and coordinating city services.

NCDC has the power to legislate on matters affecting the district, acquire and manage public assets, and coordinate urban services across the National Capital District's six urban local-level governments.

→

Established 1975

Operating under the NCDC Act as the city's governing authority.

→

Revenue & infrastructure

Funded through GST shares, property rates, business licenses, and market fees.

→

Six urban LLGs

Coordinating services across Gerehu, Waigani-University, Tokarara-Hohola, Gordons-Saraga, Boroko-Korobosea, and Kilakila-Kaugere.

→

Community-first programs

Grant funding and public seminars designed for residents and small businesses directly.
